Technical evidence & exact verification

What failed, and what Codex changed

The runner scheduled from a timestamp taken before the planner’s actual start. A fractional wait or early timer wake could leave the minimum interval unfinished. The next fixture was consumed by a local cooldown rejection.

  1. nextEligibleInMs() reads the planner’s monotonic eligibility clock.
  2. The runner rounds waits upward and checks eligibility again after every wake.
  3. Adapters mark transport initiation. Local failures do not increase the submitted-request count.
  4. local_rate_limit is separate from external rate_limit with failureSource: "provider".

The seven original zero-ms entries were attempts 15, 27, 43, 51, 61, 67, and 71. The first run also had two timeouts. None of its failed rows were silently replaced.
